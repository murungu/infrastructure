What the live review found

1. Mobile copy is not usable

The 1200 × 500 artwork renders at 338 × 141 on a 375px viewport. Headline, body, and CTA are image pixels—not responsive content.

2. The visual language is off-brand

Blue artwork, a yellow button, generic phones, and embedded typography conflict with Arity’s black, white, and structural-grey system.

3. Rotation is too aggressive

The live carousel auto-advances every 3 seconds. It has no pause control, no arrow controls, and keyboard control is disabled.

4. Semantics are incomplete

Swiper labels slide positions and bullets, but the outer carousel region has no accessible name. The whole artwork is one link, while the visible fake button is not a real control.

5. Pagination is undersized

The bullets measure 10 × 10px at all viewports. They are below the 44px target and depend on image contrast.

6. Hidden links remain focusable

With the fade effect, an inactive slide is transparent but its link stays in the keyboard sequence. Inactive slides must be hidden or inert.

7. Hero loading can improve

The first, above-the-fold image is marked lazy. Reserve its dimensions and load slide one eagerly; lazy-load only later slides.

Recommended direction to approve

  1. Campaign count: prefer one static, prioritised hero. If merchandising requires multiple campaigns, retain the manual carousel shown in this wireframe.
  2. Structure: separate headline, body, CTA, controls, and media. Never bake promotional copy or buttons into the image.
  3. Mobile: stack copy above a purpose-made crop; keep the 48px CTA visible and full width.
  4. Tablet and desktop: use a split hero with 24px tablet gutters, 32px desktop gutters, a 1600px safety cap, and a 420px target height.
  5. Brand: white/soft-grey surfaces, black text and CTA, Inter for UI copy, and no decorative red, gradients, or shadows.
  6. Content: promote renewable-energy outcomes and real catalog destinations—not generic consumer electronics.
  7. Motion: do not auto-advance by default. If autoplay is retained, use at least 8 seconds, expose Pause, stop on hover/focus, and honour reduced motion.
  8. Controls: provide 44 × 44px Previous/Next buttons, slide position, swipe support, keyboard support, and visible focus. Make every inactive slide hidden or inert.
  9. Performance: eager-load slide one with reserved dimensions; lazy-load subsequent media and supply mobile/desktop image sources.

Implementation status

Shipped in ArityTheme. The theme-owned implementation uses Views/Home/Index.cshtml, Views/Home/_ArityHomepageHero.cshtml, Content/css/arity.hero.css, and Content/js/arity.hero.js. It replaces the image-only HomepageTop Swiper. nopCommerce core remains untouched.
