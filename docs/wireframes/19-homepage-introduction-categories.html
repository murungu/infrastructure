What the live review found

1. The welcome block is default nopCommerce content

“Welcome to our store,” the online-shopping definition, and links to nopCommerce documentation and forums are not customer content. They reduce trust and repeat the job of the approved homepage hero.

2. The categories do not describe the Arity Shop catalog

Electronics, Apparel, and Digital downloads use sample imagery and send the wrong product signal. The catalog should lead with Solar panels, Batteries, Inverters, and Charge controllers.

3. Mobile scroll depth is excessive

The current single-column cards use about 200px of image height each. Customers must pass the generic topic and multiple large images before featured products appear.

4. Tablet produces an uneven 2 + 1 layout

Two cards fill the first row and the third card becomes an orphan. Four compact cards can fit in one row at 768px.

5. Each category creates two links

The current title and image are separate links to the same destination. One full-card link provides a clearer hover, touch, and keyboard target.

6. The section has no customer-oriented heading

“Shop by solution” explains why the categories are present and frames the products as parts of a complete energy system.

Recommended direction to approve

  1. Remove the HomepageText topic from the ArityTheme homepage flow. The approved hero remains the page introduction.
  2. Add a theme-owned homepage-category view. Keep nopCommerce category data dynamic, but render each category as one semantic card link.
  3. Use a 2 × 2 grid at 375px and a four-column row at 768px and 1280px. Do not hide top-level routes in a horizontal carousel.
  4. Use the hero content rail: 16px mobile gutters, 24px tablet gutters, 32px desktop gutters, and a 1600px wide-screen cap.
  5. Use white cards on a #f9f9f9 section, 1px hairline borders, 8px radius, black focus outlines, and no shadows or decorative red.
  6. Replace sample category names and imagery in the admin area. The layout must remain stable for three or four visible categories while the catalog data is updated.
  • □ Remove the generic welcome topic instead of rewriting it.
  • □ Use a visible 2 × 2 mobile grid instead of horizontal scrolling.
  • □ Use the four renewable-energy category names shown here.
  • □ Show short category descriptions on desktop only.
