## Online shopping, built around everyday life in Zimbabwe

Arity Shop is being built to make shopping online simpler and more accessible in Zimbabwe. We’re starting in Harare and surrounding areas, with the ambition to reach more communities as we grow.

Whether you’re shopping for your home, a place you rent, or a small business, our goal is straightforward: help you find what you need, order online, and have it delivered to your doorstep.

## Starting small, with room to grow

We’re at the beginning of our journey. We’re starting with a focused selection of products and building towards a broader range, learning from our customers and improving how we work along the way.

We believe online shopping should offer convenience and good value. As Arity Shop grows, we’ll work towards making it easier for you to discover products, make informed choices, and spend less time travelling from shop to shop.

## Helping local businesses reach more customers

Our vision extends beyond the products we sell. We also want to help Zimbabwean businesses reach customers online.

Many small businesses have something valuable to offer but lack the time, tools, or support to build an online store of their own. We’re looking to develop partnerships that bring those businesses and their products onto Arity Shop—helping with online visibility and, as our delivery capabilities develop, getting orders to customers.

## Building one step at a time

We know that a useful online store needs more than a website. Clear product information, reliable order handling, and delivery that customers can depend on all matter.

These are the foundations we’re working to put in place. We’ll start where we can serve customers responsibly and expand our selection and reach as our capabilities grow.

Thank you for getting to know Arity Shop. We’re excited to build this business alongside the customers and local businesses it aims to serve.
