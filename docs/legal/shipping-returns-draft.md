# Shipping & Returns — Pre-launch Draft

This file contains provisional page copy followed by internal planning notes. It is not an approved final delivery or returns policy. The owner approved the page body for local layout testing, and it was saved to Admin topic 9 after confirming system name `ShippingInfo`. Internal planning notes were not published.

## Published page body — local testing only

> **Pre-launch information — not a final policy.** Arity Shop is preparing to launch. Our delivery arrangements and returns procedures are still being developed. This page is for testing and does not confirm that delivery services are available.

### Where we plan to deliver

We plan to begin serving Harare and surrounding areas. The exact delivery areas are still being confirmed, with the intention of expanding as the business grows.

### Delivery arrangements

We are considering starting with our own small delivery team and using delivery partners as our needs develop. Delivery options will depend on the products being ordered and the areas we can serve.

Delivery charges and estimated timeframes have not yet been finalised.

### Product availability and fulfilment

We expect to begin by sourcing products from suppliers and business partners. Over time, we aim to combine partner-sourced products with stock held by Arity Shop.

The process for confirming availability and communicating delivery estimates is still being developed.

### Returns and refunds

Our procedures for damaged or faulty items, change-of-mind returns, and refunds are still being developed. We are not setting return periods, charges, exclusions, or refund timeframes in this draft.

This draft does not limit any rights customers may have under applicable law.

## Internal decision record — do not publish

### Direction supplied by the owner

These are current intentions, not operating capabilities or binding service commitments:

- Initial area: Harare and surrounding areas; exact boundaries remain to be confirmed.
- Delivery: considering own deliveries, starting with a few bikes; open to partners depending on growth.
- Delivery pricing and timing: undecided; need a model suited to the target market.
- Fulfilment: likely to source from partners initially, with a mix of partner sourcing and own stock as demand grows.
- Returns: undecided.
- Customer collection: not confirmed.

The proposed copy intentionally avoids promising bike delivery for every product. Product size, weight, safety, and handling requirements must inform the delivery arrangements, particularly for the initial solar/battery product range.

### Decisions required before real trading

- Exact delivery coverage and how serviceability is checked before checkout.
- Transport and handling for large, heavy, fragile, or otherwise restricted goods.
- Delivery charges, how they are calculated, and when customers see the total.
- Supplier stock confirmation, reservation, and lead times; whether orders are accepted or payment collected before availability is confirmed.
- Out-of-stock handling, cancellations, substitutions, and split orders.
- Delivery estimates, communications, missed deliveries, loss, and damage handling.
- Whether customer collection is offered and, if so, where and under what conditions.
- Damaged/faulty-item reporting, remedies, collection/return costs, and supplier responsibilities.
- Change-of-mind eligibility, conditions, costs, and any legally permitted exclusions.
- Refund method and timing.
- A working customer support channel and return destination.
- Review against applicable Zimbabwean consumer requirements by a qualified adviser. This draft is not evidence of legal compliance; an undecided policy does not remove statutory obligations.
