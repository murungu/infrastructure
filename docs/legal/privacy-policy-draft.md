It is based on the local pre-launch audit, not a final legal notice. `[TO CONFIRM: ...]` marks information that must be supplied or verified. ## Proposed page body

> **Pre-launch draft.** Arity Shop is preparing to launch. This notice describes the information requested by our current test store and identifies privacy arrangements that are still being finalised. It is not a final statement of our launch arrangements. Do not enter real payment-card details or other sensitive information for testing.

### Who is responsible for your information?

Arity Shop is the public name of our planned online shopping business, starting in Harare and surrounding areas, Zimbabwe.

**Business responsible for personal information:** [TO CONFIRM: legal operator name and business address.]

**Privacy contact:** [TO CONFIRM: monitored contact details and the person or team responsible for privacy enquiries.]

Our customer contact and privacy-request channels are not yet configured for launch.

### Information requested by the store

The information requested depends on the features you use:

| Feature | Information requested or configured |
| --- | --- |
| Creating an account | First name, last name, email address, password and confirmation, and a newsletter choice. The current registration form also includes optional gender and company fields. |
| Signing in or recovering an account | Login credentials or the email address used for password recovery. Phone-login controls also exist in the test site; the available launch login methods are still being confirmed. |
| Contacting us | Name, email address, and the message entered in the contact form. Email delivery from this form is not yet configured. |
| Newsletter signup | An email address and a subscription choice. The registration checkbox is not selected by default. |
| Delivery and billing | The test configuration enables address and phone fields, including optional company, second address line, and fax fields. The launch checkout fields and ordering process are still under review. |
| Browsing | Cookies that support the site and visitor recognition. The store is also configured to retain customer IP addresses; log contents and retention are still being reviewed. |

The payment arrangement is not yet finalised. A manual credit-card option remains enabled in the test configuration. **Do not use real card details on this test store.** The launch notice must identify the selected payment provider and accurately explain which payment information Arity Shop and the provider handle.

### Why information is needed

The intended purposes are to provide account access, respond to enquiries, arrange and support orders and deliveries, record newsletter choices, and maintain the site's operation and security. Not all of these services are operational yet.

[TO CONFIRM: the information actually necessary for each purpose, the applicable legal grounds for processing, and any recordkeeping obligations. Remove unused fields and purposes before launch rather than collecting information simply because the software supports it.]

### Newsletters and other optional services

Newsletter signup is optional and starts unchecked on the registration page. Mailing arrangements are still being developed.

[TO CONFIRM: the types of messages to be sent, how signup is confirmed and recorded, the email service used, and a tested way to unsubscribe before any marketing is sent.]

Google Analytics and Facebook Pixel are currently disabled in the audited local store. The Jotform chat integration is not configured to run. These observations describe the test configuration, not a guarantee about a future deployment. Any decision to enable analytics, advertising, or chat needs a review of the information involved, the notice, and any required choices before activation.

### Who receives information and where it is kept

The intended business model may involve hosting, email, payment, delivery, and product-supply partners. Final providers and their access to customer information have not been confirmed. We are not identifying potential partners as current recipients without verification.

[TO CONFIRM: actual providers or recipient categories, their purposes and access, hosting and backup locations, access by staff or suppliers, and any processing outside Zimbabwe with the applicable arrangements.]

### How long information is kept

Retention periods for accounts, guest records, orders, enquiries, logs, backups, and newsletter records have not yet been set.

[TO CONFIRM: a retention schedule or clear retention criteria, any legally required periods, and how deletion works in the application and backups. Cookie expiry is separate from retention of server-side records.]

### Protecting information

Security arrangements for the production service are still being reviewed. This notice does not claim that production safeguards are implemented or that information can be kept completely secure.

[TO CONFIRM: access controls, transport protection, credential and payment handling, backup protections, and incident-response responsibilities that can be accurately described without disclosing sensitive security details.]

### Your choices and privacy requests

You can leave the optional newsletter box unchecked and use your browser settings to manage cookies. Blocking cookies may affect site features.

Procedures for requests concerning access, correction, deletion, account closure, marketing preferences, and complaints are still being established. This draft does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.

[TO CONFIRM: applicable rights, a working request channel, identity verification, response procedures and timeframes, lawful limits on deletion, and any applicable complaint or escalation route. Do not promise unimplemented self-service tools.]

### Updates to this notice

This draft must be updated to reflect the final operating arrangements before launch. Its effective date will be added when the completed notice is approved and published.
