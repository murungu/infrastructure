## How to publish

1. Draft the content in Markdown here.
2. Review with Arity legal team.
3. Copy approved text into admin panel:
   - Admin → Content Management → Topics → [Topic Name]
4. Verify the page renders correctly at 375/768/1280px.
5. Mark as complete in `../branding/WORKSHEET.md`.

## South Africa legal context

- **POPIA**: Protection of Personal Information Act — requires Privacy Policy
- **CPA**: Consumer Protection Act — requires Terms of Sale, returns policy
- **ECTA**: Electronic Communications and Transactions Act — requires business identification
- **VAT**: Must display VAT-inclusive pricing if turnover > R1M

See `../branding/WORKSHEET.md` Section: Legal Pages for full requirements.
