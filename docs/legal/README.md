## Zimbabwe legal context

| Law | Applies To | What it means for Arity Shop |
|---|---|---|
| **Cyber Security and Data Protection Act (2021)** | All Zimbabwean businesses | Must have Privacy Policy. Must explain data collection, storage, sharing. Must allow deletion requests. Must report breaches. |
| **Consumer Protection Act (2019)** | All Zim businesses selling to consumers | Must display Terms of Sale. Must allow returns within reasonable period. Must display pricing clearly. No unfair contract terms. |
| **VAT Act** | Businesses with turnover > threshold | Must display VAT-inclusive pricing (15%). Must issue tax invoices. Register with ZIMRA if threshold met. |
| **IMTT (2%)** | Electronic transactions | 2% Intermediated Money Transfer Tax on mobile money and electronic payments. Factor into pricing or absorb. |
| **Exchange Control** | USD transactions | Ensure pricing and invoicing comply with Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe exchange control regulations. |

## Currency and pricing notes

- All prices in **USD** (primary transactional currency in Zimbabwe)
- Display **VAT-inclusive pricing** (15%)
- Disclose **IMTT** (2%) where applicable
- Show **shipping costs upfront** (Harare $3-7, Bulawayo $5-10, rural $10-25 USD)
- Offer **EcoCash** and **Cash on Delivery** for trust
