## How to publish

1. Draft the content in Markdown here.
2. Resolve draft markers, verify the intended deployment, and obtain owner approval and appropriate qualified legal review.
3. Copy approved text into admin panel:
   - Admin → Content Management → Topics → [Topic Name]
4. Verify the page renders correctly at 375/768/1280px.
5. Mark as complete in `../branding/WORKSHEET.md`.

## Jurisdiction and approval scope

The confirmed market is Zimbabwe, initially Harare and surrounding areas. Establish the legal operator and applicable privacy, consumer, electronic-commerce, recordkeeping, and cross-border requirements with a qualified adviser. This directory does not establish legal compliance.

Older South Africa-specific notes in `../branding/WORKSHEET.md` are historical context, not a validated requirements list for this business. Do not copy their legal or tax assumptions into these drafts.

The owner reviewed and approved the wording as working pre-launch drafts and authorized committing this content package, explicitly acknowledging it is not production-ready. That approval does not authorize further publication or launch. Git preserves these files, not the local nopCommerce database settings; use the content and configuration records when separately approving changes on another instance.
